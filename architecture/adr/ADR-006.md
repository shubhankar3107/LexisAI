ADR-006

Decision

We’ll use a two-phase deletion strategy.

Phase 1

Business deletion.

ACTIVE
↓
SOFT_DELETED

The document disappears from the UI.

⸻

Phase 2

Infrastructure cleanup.

Cleanup Job
↓
Delete physical assets
↓
Hard delete database records

⸻

V1

We won’t build a background cleanup worker yet.

Instead:

* We’ll support soft deletion.
* Physical cleanup can be triggered manually or by a simple maintenance command during development.

When we reach V5 and introduce background processing (Celery, Redis, etc.), we’ll replace that with an automated cleanup pipeline.

This is a perfect example of our philosophy:

Design for the future, implement only what’s necessary today.
