ADR 28 — Soft Delete by Default

Status

Accepted

⸻

Context

Documents may be referenced by audit logs, future features, or legal workflows. Permanently deleting rows would remove historical information and make recovery impossible.

⸻

Decision

Documents will use soft deletion.

Deleting a document sets:

to the current UTC timestamp.

Repository queries will automatically exclude soft-deleted rows unless a method explicitly requests otherwise.

⸻

Rationale

* Preserves audit history.
* Allows future recovery.
* Prevents accidental data loss.
* Aligns with common enterprise practices.
